# Third-party notices

NextGuard's Python and shell implementation is original MIT-licensed work. It contains no copied third-party program and no bundled IP-list data.

At runtime, an administrator may enable downloads from the providers listed in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Those files remain subject to their providers' copyrights, database rights, access policies, attribution requirements, rate limits, and other terms. NextGuard stores them locally solely to operate the administrator's firewall. It does not publish a combined feed.

Required names/attribution retained in source metadata and status/event provenance include Spamhaus DROP, blocklist.de, SANS Internet Storm Center/DShield, IPsum, OpenFilters, Censys, Shodan, and CyberOK/Skipa. Use of a name identifies provenance and does not imply endorsement.

The project uses Python's standard library and system-provided systemd, iptables, ipset, journald, and logrotate. These are external dependencies, not redistributed in this repository. Distribution packages carry their own notices.
